Exactly one word - the word a grandma would text about AI - entered in make.fun's 'One Word From Grandma' bounty. The word is the whole entry; the program writes it to `/output/entry.txt`:

> AIn't

It is her dialect and the subject in a single breath: the word she would actually type, warm and a little baffled, with AI sitting right inside it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
